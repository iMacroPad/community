Your own pad in numbers, drawn like Settings ▸ Stats and in your theme's colours.

## What you get

- **Today**: screen time and taps, with the week as a sparkline.
- **Screen time chart**: the last 14 days, with your daily average.
- **Time of day**: the hours you use the pad most.
- **Most used** layouts, Mac apps and themes, as rings.
- **Most pressed keys**.
- A **Stats dashboard** panel that puts the main numbers together.
- Two ready layouts: *Stats* and *Stats · keys & themes*. Every panel can go on any layout or on the screensaver.

## Good to know

- Everything is counted on this iPad and never leaves it. There is nothing to set up and no permission to grant.
- The numbers grow as you use the pad: a new install starts empty. The screenshots show sample stats.
