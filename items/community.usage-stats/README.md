Your own pad in numbers, on any layout or the screensaver.

## What you get

- **Today**: screen time and taps, with your top layouts.
- **This week**: screen time per day as a bar chart.
- **Leaderboard**: the layouts, Mac apps and themes you use most.
- **Top keys**: the keys you press most.

Everything is read from Settings ▸ Stats on this iPad. Nothing leaves it.
