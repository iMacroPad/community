Your teams in their own colours, on your pad: club cards, match countdowns, scoreboards, full league tables, player
cards, tennis rankings and Formula 1. Any sport and any country.

## What you get

Six ready layouts, each a page of the app's own sports panels:

- **Formula 1**: the next race with a live countdown and every session time in your time zone, driver cards
  with their photos (Lewis Hamilton, Charles Leclerc), a team card with the car (Ferrari), and the drivers' and constructors' standings.
- **Football · Süper Lig**: Galatasaray's club card with the next and last match, a countdown to the next match,
  the last result as a scoreboard, the full league table and a player card (Victor Osimhen).
- **Football · Europe**: full Premier League, LaLiga and Champions League tables, and Real Madrid's card.
- **US leagues**: NFL, NHL and NBA standings by conference, and the Chiefs' last game.
- **Tennis**: the ATP and WTA rankings.
- **Around the world**: Lakers, Maple Leafs, MLS, Halkbank Ankara volleyball and more.

24 panels in all, to add to any layout: team card, next match, last result, league table, player card, F1 next
race, F1 drivers, F1 constructors, F1 driver and team cards, ATP and WTA rankings. Each panel has five looks:
club colours, photo, scoreboard, minimal or automatic.

## Set your own team

Nobody needs to look up an ID. Press **Edit**, select a panel and choose **Team**, **League** or **Player**:
search by name, or browse sport ▸ country ▸ league. F1 panels have a **Driver** or **Team** picker.

## Where the data comes from

The panels fetch from the iPad itself, with no account and no key:
[TheSportsDB](https://www.thesportsdb.com) (teams, players, any league),
ESPN's public scoreboards (full tables for the big leagues) and
[Jolpica](https://github.com/jolpica/jolpica-f1) (Formula 1). Only the team, league or player you picked is sent.
TheSportsDB's free feed lists up to ten teams of a league; search by name for any other.

It asks for no permissions: it never presses a key on your Mac.
