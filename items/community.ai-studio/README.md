Keys that send the text on your clipboard to AI services and bring the answer back to your Mac.

## What you get

- **Image from clipboard**: sends your clipboard text to Gemini 2.5 Flash Image and saves the picture to Downloads/iMacroPad.
- **Answer the clipboard**: asks Gemini 2.5 Flash and puts a short answer on your clipboard.
- **Video (MCP)**: asks a video tool on an MCP server and opens the result.
- **Your Shortcut**: runs one of your own Shortcuts by name.
- The *AI Studio Neon* theme.

## Before you start

1. Put your Gemini API key into the Mac's keychain as `GEMINI_API_KEY`. The file never holds a key.
2. Install, then switch on only the permissions you want. What stays off is removed from your copy.

Nothing is sent until you press a key.
