Your team on the pad: the next match, the last scores and the league table.

## What you get

- **F1**: your team's standing in the constructors' championship, your driver's standing, the next race with practice and qualifying in your own time zone, and the drivers' table.
- **Football, volleyball, NHL, NFL and more**: next match, last result, recent results and league tables.

## Set your team

1. Put a widget on a layout.
2. **Edit ▸ the widget ▸ Live data** and enter the team, driver or league.
3. F1 teams and drivers are written as Jolpica IDs: `ferrari`, `mclaren`, `red_bull`; `hamilton`, `leclerc`, `max_verstappen`.
4. Other team IDs are in the address of the team's page on thesportsdb.com.

Data comes from TheSportsDB's free feed, ESPN and Jolpica (F1). The Mac fetches it while the widget is on screen; the pad never calls these servers itself.
