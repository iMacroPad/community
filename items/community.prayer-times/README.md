Prayer times on your pad and in the screensaver, worked out on the iPad or taken from Diyanet's official list.

## What you get

- **Next prayer**: its name and time, and the time left as a clock (1:05:09) or in words (1 hour 5 minutes),
  with the next makruh (kerahat) window underneath.
- **Prayer times today**: the six times (Fajr/İmsak, Sunrise/Güneş, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha) with the Hijri date;
  the current one is marked and the next one counts down.
- **Kerahat times**: how long until the next makruh window, or until the one you are in ends: after sunrise,
  at the zenith (istiwa) and before sunset.
- **Day circle**: sun, twilight and night on one ring, every time numbered on it, the makruh windows in red and the
  sun (or the moon) where it is now.
- A **Prayer board** layout, a **Prayer times** screensaver scene, and six panels in the widget list to put on
  any layout or scene. The app also has a ready **Prayer Times** scene in Settings ▸ Screensaver.

## Make it yours

Press **Edit** and select a panel:

- **Place**: this iPad's location, a city or district search, or Diyanet's list by country, state / province and
  city / district. Without one the panel uses the city chosen in Settings ▸ Screensaver.
- **Method**: Diyanet's official list for the district, or one of 20 calculations (Diyanet, Muslim World League,
  ISNA, Egypt, Umm al-Qura, Karachi, Tehran, Jafari, Gulf, Kuwait, Qatar, Dubai, Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia,
  France, Russia, Tunisia, Algeria, Morocco). **Asr**: standard or Hanafi.
- **Temkin**: minutes added to each of the six times. **Kerahat**: the length of each makruh window (45 minutes each
  unless you change it).
- **Remind**: at the time or up to an hour before, for the prayers you pick, and when a makruh window begins.
  The Next prayer panel on the Prayer board reminds 15 minutes before; the other panels do not remind.
- **Colours**: the panel turns red in a makruh window and orange while a reminder's lead time runs; pick your own.
- **Language**: Turkish, English, German, French or Arabic, or the iPad's.

## Good to know

- Needs iMacroPad with the prayer panels (Settings ▸ Screensaver lists a Prayer Times scene).
- The calculated times are worked out on the iPad, with no network. The official list comes from
  ezanvakti.emushaf.net (Diyanet's published times) for the chosen district, about once a month; a place search
  goes to Open-Meteo's geocoding. Nothing else is sent anywhere.
- Reminders are notifications on this iPad and ask for permission the first time. No permission in the file.
